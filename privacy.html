StockWise

Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 October 2026

StockWise is a stock-keeping tool for restaurants. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing you enter is sold or shared with third parties for their own use.

What the app stores

  • The name you type in when you first open the app, so stock changes show who made them.
  • Stock records you create: items, quantities, prices, suppliers and their contact details, orders, stock checks and the history of changes.
  • Settings such as the restaurant name, currency and manager PIN.

The app does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or any account login.

Where it is stored

By default everything stays on your device, in the app's private storage. It never leaves the phone unless you export it yourself.

If your restaurant turns on live sync, the same records are kept in a Google Firebase database that belongs to your restaurant, so staff phones share one stock list. That database is controlled by whoever set it up for your restaurant, and Google processes it under the Firebase privacy terms.

Internet use

  • Text fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your device's IP address as part of a normal web request.
  • "Open" buttons send you to supplier websites or apps you have chosen. Those sites have their own privacy policies.
  • Live sync, when enabled, connects to Firebase as described above.

Exports and sharing

CSV exports, backups and order messages are created only when you tap the button, and go only where you send them (for example WhatsApp, email or a file).

Deleting your data

Use More → Delete all data in the app, or uninstall it, to remove everything stored on the device. For a live-sync database, the restaurant's administrator can delete the data from the Firebase console.

Children

StockWise is a workplace tool meant for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes
